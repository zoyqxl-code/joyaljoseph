About Me

The Story

I'm Joyal Joseph — a multi-disciplinary creative and entrepreneur based in India. My journey spans across videography, video editing, 3D art, VFX, and drone cinematography.

I bring stories to life through the lens — from cinematic short films and brand videos to immersive 3D renders and visual effects. On the business side, I'm building ventures in e-commerce and export-import, blending creative vision with entrepreneurial ambition.

Currently exploring Houdini and pushing the boundaries of procedural art while sharpening my skills in Final Cut Pro.
